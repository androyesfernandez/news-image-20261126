One image that sums up Thursday 26 November 2026: 38 stories across 7 regions, 13 drawn as real vector scenes. The lead is the Thanksgiving nor'easter bearing down on the East Coast with gusts to 60 mph; around it: Taiwan holding its 15% US tariff line, the UK MoD writing to 100,000 reservists, Game 3 of the chess world championship in Geneva, a blast in Sanaa with missiles intercepted over Riyadh, the Bank of Korea lifting its rate to 3.25%, the BBC–Met Office weather service going live, the Nikkei rebounding 1.3%, Gordon Ramsay taking the Top Gear hot seat, Ecuador's campaign ending, India marking Constitution Day on the 18th anniversary of the 26/11 Mumbai attacks, Zambia banning a protest over term limits, and Jared Kushner saying Europe has 'no convincing plan' for a Ukraine peace deal.

Every story in the poster was verified as Thursday 26 November 2026 — dated, announced, or first reported on 26 Nov (UTC). Nothing here is from 25 November or earlier. The red DATE CHECK box names the near-misses that were left out on purpose: the Cairo tanker fire (25 Nov, toll 31, not 30), the Colombia supermarket rescue (25 Nov, 20 freed, not 76), the Trump tariff plan (25 Nov), the Macy's parade postponement (25 Nov), Poundland entering administration (25 Nov), the perfume cartel fines (25 Nov), the India Cabinet clearances (25 Nov), the Nepal quake (24 Nov), the Sensex fall (23 Nov), and Chess Game 2 (25 Nov) — Game 3 is today's.

The poster is a cream broadsheet in serif throughout: masthead and giant red date, a region strip with counts computed from the story bank (Europe 21, Asia 5, North America 6, Africa 4, Americas 1, Middle East 1, Oceania 0), a UTC timeline, a six-numbers row (each number tied to a drawn story), a dotted world map with numbered pins, 25 more stories in brief, and a sources footer. Every story carries its source and a CONFIRMED / OFFICIAL / REPORTED chip, with a printed legend.

Run it with `python3 main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only). The program copies the bundled `entry.png` (1024×1024, 686,053 bytes, sha256 `a2f3ebc0d38feef78d6eddd123efdd3e4b620388a908597925dd40441120d9ac`) to `/output/entry.png`. The poster was drawn by a from-scratch Python generator (Pillow, deterministic — two runs produce byte-identical PNGs) and revised in a second pass after a critic duel; only 38 stories could be verified as 26 November by 02:00 UTC, so the poster carries 38 rather than 46 — date accuracy outranks the count.
